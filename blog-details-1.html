THE LIVING RUST:
CORTEN EVOLUTION

THE PERSISTENCE OF OXIDIZED STEEL

Corten steel, also known as weathering steel, represents a unique paradox in industrial art. While most metals are finished to resist oxidation, Corten is designed to do exactly the opposite. Its chemical composition allows it to develop a stable, rust-like appearance after exposure to the elements for several years.

"RUST IS NOT DECAY; IT IS THE SCULPTURE'S ADAPTATION TO ITS ENVIRONMENT."

When used in large-scale public art, Corten offers a living aesthetic. A sculpture installed in early spring might look deep burnt-orange, only to shift towards a darker, purplish-brown after a particularly heavy rainy season. This evolution creates a bond between the object and the environment that few other materials can claim.

Structural Longevity

Contrary to appearances, the rust layer is actually a protective barrier. It slows down the corrosion process significantly, ensuring that the structural integrity of the steel remains intact for decades, even in coastal environments where salt levels are high. At Forge & Form, we source our Corten from select mills to ensure the highest purity and most dramatic weathering patterns.
