THE HEAT TREATMENT:
PRECISION WELDING

THE STRUCTURAL SOUL OF MONUMENTAL ART

In industrial sculpture, the weld is more than just a connection—it's a signature. High-grade structural welding requires a mastery of heat control, especially when dealing with massive steel plates that can warp or lose their temper if the arc isn't perfectly managed. At Forge & Form, we treat every bead as a critical component of the work's longevity and aesthetic flow.

"A PERFECT WELD IS INVISIBLE TO THE EYE BUT INDESTRUCTIBLE TO THE ELEMENTS."

We utilize specialized TIG and MIG welding techniques tailored specifically for the materials we use. Whether it's the deep penetration required for structural 12mm steel or the delicate, aesthetic joins on polished stainless components, our master welders operate with sub-millimeter precision. Each join is then painstakingly ground and finished to match the surrounding material's grain.

Structural Integrity

Beyond the surface, every weld must undergo rigorous inspection. For projects destined for high-traffic public spaces or coastal environments, we perform non-destructive testing to ensure there are no occlusions or stress points. This commitment to 'The Heat Treatment' is what allows our sculptures to stand defiant against gravity and the passage of time, decade after decade.
