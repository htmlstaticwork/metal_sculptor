MONOCHROME VOID

An experiment in absence. By applying a matte, ultra-black powder coating to complex steel geometry, Monochrome Void absorbs light and confounds depth perception, challenging the viewer to find the physical perimeter of the object.

DIMENSIONS 3m Height x 3m Width
MATERIAL Powder Coated Steel
LOCATION Minimalist Art Fair, Berlin
CLIENT Speculative Exhibition
