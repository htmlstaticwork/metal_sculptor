THE PHILOSOPHY OF ARTHUR STEEL

Arthur Steel is a master metal sculptor with over 20 years of experience in heavy industrial fabrication. His work focuses on the intersection of industrial strength and organic fluidity, often exploring how massive, unyielding materials like corten steel can respond to natural environments.

"METAL IS NOT DEAF. IT RESONATES WITH THE SPACE IT OCCUPIES."

Based in Steel Valley, CA, Arthur partners with world-renowned architects to create site-specific installations that challenge the scale of contemporary public art. His work can be found in museum collections, high-end corporate parks, and private landscape projects across three continents.
