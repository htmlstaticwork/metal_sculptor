MATERIAL INTEGRITY

We believe the material dictates the form. Our studio specializes in three primary alloys, each chosen for its unique elemental behavior capacity.

OXIDIZED CORTEN

A living metal that evolves with its environment, developing a protective, burnt patina over time.

STAINLESS STEEL

Polished to a mirror finish or directionally brushed, designed to reflect the architectural spaces.

FORGED IRON

The brutalist foundation. Raw, heavy, and visceral—shaped by immense heat and sheer physical force.
